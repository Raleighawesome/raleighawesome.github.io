Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new odometer reading?

Tap the green "+" button on the dashboard to open the quick entry screen. Enter your current odometer reading and optionally add a note.

How is my pace calculated?

OnPace Lease calculates your daily allowance based on your lease terms. It compares how much you've driven against how much you should have used by today to stay on pace.

Why does my widget show "No Vehicle"?

Open the main app and complete the onboarding setup first. The widget will update within 30 minutes, or you can add/remove the widget to refresh it immediately.

How do I sync my data across devices?

Make sure you're signed into iCloud on all devices and iCloud sync is enabled in the app settings. Your data will automatically sync.

Can I use kilometers instead of miles?

Yes! Go to Settings and toggle the unit preference between miles and kilometers.

Can I track multiple vehicles?

Multiple vehicle support is coming in a future update. Currently, the app tracks one vehicle at a time.

How do I delete my data?

You can delete all your data by deleting the app from your device. If you have iCloud sync enabled, also go to Settings > Apple ID > iCloud > Manage Storage to remove cloud data.
